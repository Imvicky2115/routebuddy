Commuter Safety Guidelines

⚠️ Notice: RideSplit™ is an open community board. Users are not identity-verified by background checks. Exercise standard caution before riding.

1. Verify Plates: Pillions must match the number plate on the app with the physical vehicle before sitting.

2. Mandatory ISI Helmets: MVA Section 129 requires BIS/ISI certified helmets for both rider and pillion.

3. Never Board if Uncomfortable: If a commuter seems rash or inebriated, cancel immediately and dial 112.

4. 100% Online UPI Split: Avoid cash disputes. Fuel splits are strictly capped at max ₹25.
