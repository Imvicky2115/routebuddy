Safety & Pilot Notice

Community Pilot Notice

Profiles are currently community-submitted and not government-verified. Always inspect the vehicle plate and coordinate on WhatsApp before boarding.

1. Mandatory Helmets

Motor Vehicles Act requires certified ISI/BIS helmets for both rider and pillion.

2. Dynamic QR Pass

Scan rider or passenger pass before departure to confirm you are paired correctly.

3. Direct UPI Fuel Share

Contribute exact fuel split directly to the rider's personal UPI ID with zero cash disputes.

Commuter FAQs

Are profiles identity-verified?

We are currently in open community pilot mode. Identity KYC is actively under development, so commuters should verify peer credentials independently.

How do I verify boarding?

Display your QR pass on screen. The rider scans it in 1 second using the built-in scanner.

How does fuel contribution work?

Tapping 'Pay via UPI' opens Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm pre-filled for the rider's UPI ID.
