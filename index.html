Commuter Safety Rules

1. Mandatory ISI Helmets: Both rider and pillion must wear BIS/ISI compliant helmets across Pune.

2. 4-Digit PIN: Pillion shares the boarding verification PIN with the rider before departure.

3. Section 66 MVA: Non-commercial fuel contribution (₹10–₹25 max) with zero commercial taxi markup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay? Tapping 'Pay via UPI' opens Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm pre-filled for fuel share.

How do I chat? Tap the WhatsApp button to coordinate pickup with your route buddy.

How do I verify boarding? Show the 4-digit PIN on your active booking banner to the rider.
