Commuter Safety Rules

1. Mandatory ISI Helmets: Both rider and pillion must wear BIS/ISI compliant helmets across Pune corridors.

2. 4-Digit Verification PIN: Pillion shares the boarding verification PIN with the rider before departure.

3. Section 66 MVA Compliance: Strict peer-to-peer fuel cost sharing (₹10–₹25 max) with zero commercial taxi markup.

4. Strict Commute Locking: Bookings cannot be cancelled by pillions to prevent leaving pooled riders stranded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why are phone numbers hidden? To safeguard your privacy, direct mobile numbers are hidden. Confirmed commuters coordinate pickup via WhatsApp with +91 pre-filled.

What payment methods are supported? You can pay directly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) or handover exact Cash (₹10–₹25 max) to your co-rider at drop-off.

What happens when departure time passes? Unbooked rides expire instantly at departure time to ensure search feeds stay 100% active and accurate.
