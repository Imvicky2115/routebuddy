Commuter Safety Rules

1. Mandatory Helmets: MVA Section 129 mandates BIS/ISI helmets for both rider and pillion.

2. Instant QR Boarding: Rider scans the passenger's dynamic pass to start the commute.

3. 100% Online UPI Payment: Direct fuel share to rider via UPI to eliminate disputes.

4. Strict Commute Locking: Bookings cannot be cancelled by pillions once confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify boarding? Show the dynamic QR code on your active pass to the rider. The rider scans it in 1 second.

How do I pay? Tapping 'Pay via UPI' opens Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm pre-filled for the rider's UPI ID.

When do unbooked rides expire? Unbooked listings vanish automatically at departure time.
