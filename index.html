Commuter Safety Rules

1. Mandatory ISI Helmets: Both rider and pillion must wear BIS/ISI compliant helmets across Pune.

2. 4-Digit PIN: Pillion shares the booking verification PIN with the rider before departure.

3. Section 66 MVA: Non-commercial fuel contribution (₹10–₹25 max) with zero commercial taxi markup.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pay? Tapping 'Pay via UPI' opens Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm pre-filled for ₹15 fuel share.

How do I cancel? The rider who listed the seat can tap 'Cancel Listing' on their card anytime.
