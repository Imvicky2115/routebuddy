Commuter Safety Rules

1. Mandatory ISI Helmets Both rider and pillion must wear BIS/ISI compliant helmets on all Pune roads.
2. 4-Digit Verification Confirm the rider's identity and share the 4-digit security PIN before boarding.
3. Women-Only Matching Dedicated matching filter for female tech corridor commuters.
4. Non-Commercial Cost Sharing Capped at ₹10–₹25 fuel share under Section 66 MVA.

Commuter FAQs

Is bike pooling legal in Maharashtra?

Yes, non-commercial peer pooling where riders share fuel expenses without generating profits is permitted under Section 66 MVA.

How do I pay fuel share?

Directly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) to the rider when boarding.
