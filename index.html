Mandatory Safety Rules

1. ISI Helmets: MVA Sec 129 mandates BIS/ISI certified helmets for both rider and pillion.

2. Instant QR Boarding: Always scan passenger's pass before vehicle starts moving.

3. Transparent UPI: Fixed max ₹25 petrol split contribution paid straight to rider via UPI.

4. Confirmed Seats: Prevents last-minute ghosting on daily Pune office lanes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send fuel contribution? Tap the UPI link on your pass to open GPay, PhonePe, or Paytm with the rider's ID and exact amount preloaded.
